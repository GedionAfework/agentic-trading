1. Purpose and Scope
This SRS defines the complete product requirements for a private, self-hosted AI trading copilot that ingests the
owner's trading courses, strategies, screenshots and guides; monitors configured markets; identifies candidate
setups; answers questions; analyzes chart screenshots; applies deterministic strategy and risk rules; sends
Telegram/mobile alerts; and records outcomes for later analytics. The system is designed as decision support and
research tooling. It must not imply guaranteed profitability or certainty about future price movement.
1.1 Product Goals
 Keep the primary LLM, vision model, embeddings, knowledge base, prompts, agent memory and trading
journal on infrastructure controlled by the owner.
 Translate informal trading knowledge into versioned, testable strategy definitions and deterministic rule
checks.
 Continuously scan selected markets without continuously invoking the LLM for every tick.
 Provide explainable setup alerts that cite the strategy/rules that were satisfied and those that were not.
 Allow natural-language and screenshot-based interaction through both a mobile application and a Telegram
bot.
 Maintain a complete evidence trail for each setup: source candles, derived indicators, rule results, model
outputs, risk assessment, notifications and final outcome.
 Support backtesting and paper trading before any future consideration of live broker execution.
1.2 Out of Scope for Baseline Release
 Autonomous live-money order placement.
 Guaranteed buy/sell predictions or profitability claims.
 High-frequency or ultra-low-latency trading.
 Custody of user funds or acting as a broker/exchange.
 Training a foundation model from scratch.
 Social copy-trading, public signal marketplace, or multi-tenant public SaaS in baseline.

The system separates probabilistic AI tasks from deterministic financial calculations. LLM/VLM components may
retrieve, summarize, classify, explain and propose interpretations. Numeric indicator calculation, risk/reward
calculation, strategy predicates, data freshness checks, entitlement checks, signal state transitions and
notification deduplication must be implemented in deterministic code.
